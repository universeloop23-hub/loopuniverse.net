Our History

From a small lab to a global powerhouse.

Founded in 2018, Loop Universe began as a specialized software laboratory focusing on extreme UI performance. Our mission was simple: eliminate the friction between humans and machines through superior design engineering.

Today, we partner with industry leaders and disruptive startups to build products that don't just work—they inspire. We have maintained our small-team agility while scaling our technical capabilities to support millions of concurrent users across our enterprise solutions.
